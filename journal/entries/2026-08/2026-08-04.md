### Summary
The developer documented a critical defect discovered during the sixth review round of the claude-config audit: two rule files (`rules/hooks-reference.md` and `rules/bats-bash-testing.md`) are configured to load through both mechanisms simultaneously — they carry `paths:` frontmatter *and* are `@`-referenced from the project's own `.claude/CLAUDE.md` file. This is the exact arrangement the project's rules explicitly forbid, since it loads the same content twice.

The core problem is that both tools built to catch this violation are blind to it. The checker (`check-rule-frontmatter.sh`) and the inventory script (`measure-context-load.sh`) both derive the set of `@`-referenced files by reading only `global/CLAUDE.md`, missing the references that live in the project's separate `CLAUDE.md`. As a result, the checker reports "All rules have exactly one loading mechanism" while the inventory reports both files as on-demand and non-compacting — both false. The defect exposes approximately 10,937 bytes classified incorrectly, roughly 17% higher than the recorded always-loaded total of 63,619 bytes, and in the unsafe direction for the byte budget.

The developer documented this as the third instance of a broader pattern the audit has been uncovering: checks passing confidently over real violations. In this case, the check cannot see the file where the violation lives. The developer recorded that teaching both tools to scan every `CLAUDE.md` is the correct fix (the "derive" remedy) and is the responsibility of the upcoming Milestone A4. The per-file remedy — whether to drop the `@`-reference or drop the `paths:` frontmatter — is deferred as a deliberate decision rather than a cleanup task.

The developer also noted an unresolved question: the compaction probe produced no `include` record for either import from the project `CLAUDE.md`, while all twelve user-level imports returned. Whether project-level imports are re-resolved after compaction, load only at session start, or fail to resolve entirely remains unsettled and requires another measurement run. The developer updated the research documentation to reflect that the compaction findings cover user-level imports only, and added a caveat stating this gap explicitly rather than leaving it assumed.
