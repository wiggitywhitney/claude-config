### Summary
The developer fixed several issues that came up during review of PRD #109 work. The symlink checker was incorrectly reporting dangling links as LIVE when they had no personal skill shadowing them — it was calling a skill that resolves to nothing as taking effect. The developer added a distinct BROKEN verdict, checked for dangling links first before evaluating shadows, and wrote a failing test to confirm the fix works.

The card-making skill was diffing HEAD instead of the branch it had just resolved in the previous step, which meant if the checkout didn't match, the cards would describe unrelated work. The developer updated the instructions to be explicit about diffing the resolved branch, not whatever happens to be checked out, and added a note warning that silently diffing HEAD would produce cards with no way to detect the mismatch.

Two decisions that were recorded in the decision log never made it into the milestone body they're supposed to govern: Decision 80 about a seventh upstream skill (`prd-queue`), and Decision 78 about the full consumer count being sixteen repos with 128 symlinks instead of just nine. The developer updated the success criteria and task descriptions to reflect both of these.

The research document about the three-way skill comparison got dated for its amendments and the truncation claim was marked as estimated rather than measured, since it was based on reasoning about where the cut would happen, not an actual measurement. The developer also fixed a changelog entry that claimed no behavior change for a skill that actually lost a step in the same release — it now accurately notes that the removal changed nothing but the card-making step did change and is documented separately.
