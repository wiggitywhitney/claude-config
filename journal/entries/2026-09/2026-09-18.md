### Summary
The developer updated the global prompt guidelines to require that every question presented to them include the pros and cons of each option alongside a stated recommendation.

This came from observing a pattern where presenting bare choices forced the developer to do the analysis work themselves, then ask for it as a follow-up step. By folding the analysis into the initial question, the AI could give more useful guidance upfront. The developer added an exemption for trivial confirmations where the tradeoff is self-evident, keeping the rule practical and avoiding boilerplate.

The changes went into two places: the main prompt file (`global/CLAUDE.md`) got a new section under "Presenting Options" that spells out the requirement, and the progress log documented the decision with context about why it matters. The developer also noted in the progress log that this rule applies whether there's one question or several, and emphasized that the analysis should be part of asking, not a follow-up.
