In `shell.md`, two awk-related traps were captured: macOS's default awk doesn't support gawk's three-argument `match()` form for array capture, requiring a fallback to the POSIX two-step approach using `RSTART` and `RLENGTH`; and `set -o pipefail` causes variable assignment to abort if any pipeline stage fails, even when the final stage succeeds, requiring explicit guards on each stage that might match nothing. Both gotchas were tied to real incidents in recent log-monitoring and evaluation scripts.

In `web-tree-sitter-gotchas.md`, two Node stability issues were documented: Node objects aren't referentially stable across accessor calls, so identity checks with `===` always fail and must use `.startIndex` comparison instead; and `try_statement` blocks expose their body as a field while `except`/`else`/`finally` clauses expose theirs only as named children, requiring different traversal logic. Both were verified against tree-sitter-python 0.25.0 and web-tree-sitter 0.27.0, with one caught during a CodeRabbit review.

In `writing-voice.md`, two voice rules were added: answer only what the recipient is waiting on rather than padding emails with irrelevant personal context, and never use "nobody" or "no one" as a phrasing choice—both are AI tells that should be replaced with concrete subjects.
