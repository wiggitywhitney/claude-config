## Key Decisions

- **`UserPromptSubmit` hook capabilities clarified**: The hook can block prompts or attach additional context, but cannot rewrite prompt text. This capability is confirmed for the CLI/interactive path; other channels (IDE/Desktop, MCP-originated prompts) were not verified. The hook was not present in version 2.1.221, is confirmed present in 2.1.278, but the exact introduction version is unknown.

- **Project-level `@`-imports never resolve into context**: A fresh-session probe on 2026-09-21 definitively established that project-level imports from `.claude/CLAUDE.md` do not resolve into context at session start or after compaction. This means import content costs nothing toward the byte budget and should be excluded entirely from Milestone C1 calculations.

- **`autoMode.environment` cannot extend the fixed classifier-block budget**: The pause thresholds (3 consecutive or 20 total blocks) are fixed and non-configurable. `autoMode.environment` only reduces false-positive blocks before that budget is spent by naming trusted infrastructure to the classifier. For unattended runs, populate this setting to reduce false alarms, but don't expect it alone to make a task survive repeated legitimate blocks.

- **Applied CodeRabbit triage rubric to documentation findings**: Used the Fix/Defer/Skip rubric (Fix if real and effort is the only barrier; Defer if complex; Skip if misunderstood). Applied five fixes to Milestone B1 documentation; skipped journal entries per standing policy against editing auto-generated prose, and skipped one finding that misunderstood the document's correction convention.
